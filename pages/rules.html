OFFICIAL COMMUNITY POLICY

COMMUNITY RULES

These rules are created to keep NIGHTFALL DEVILS a safe, respectful, united, and supportive community for everyone.

👿 NIGHTFALL DEVILS • Established October 18, 2026

OUR COMMUNITY STANDARD

Being part of NIGHTFALL DEVILS means choosing friendship, respect, support, privacy, and unity. Every member is expected to follow these rules both inside and outside community activities when representing NIGHTFALL DEVILS.

01

Respect Everyone

Treat all members, streamers, viewers, supporters, and other communities with respect. Different opinions are allowed, but disrespect is not.

02

No Harassment or Personal Attacks

Personal attacks, bullying, harassment, insults, threats, humiliation, or intentionally hurting another member are not allowed.

03

No Below-the-Belt Comments

Avoid comments or actions intended to embarrass, shame, provoke, or attack someone personally. Keep conversations respectful.

04

Protect Personal Information

Never disclose, share, post, or distribute another member's personal information without permission.

05

Keep Private Matters Private

Personal issues, disagreements, and sensitive matters should be handled privately and respectfully instead of being spread publicly.

06

Do Not Involve Other Communities

NIGHTFALL DEVILS community issues should not be used to create conflict with other groups, teams, or communities.

07

Support, Don't Bring Down

Support our members and streamers through positive engagement. Do not attack, insult, or intentionally bring down other creators.

08

No Unnecessary Drama

Avoid creating arguments, spreading rumors, provoking conflict, or turning small disagreements into community problems.

09

Membership Requires Approval

New members must be referred to Admin and reviewed before being officially added to the community.

10

No Approval = No Adding

Do not add or invite someone as an official member without Admin approval. Please wait until the request has been reviewed.

11

No Mandatory Payment or Gifts

Membership does not require payment, coins, gifts, or any mandatory financial contribution. Any support should always be voluntary.

12

Follow TikTok and Platform Rules

All members must follow TikTok Community Guidelines and other applicable platform rules while participating in community activities.

13

Handle Disputes Properly

If a problem happens between members, report it to Admin privately when necessary. Avoid public arguments and unnecessary escalation.

14

Respect Admin Decisions

Admins are responsible for reviewing membership requests, maintaining order, and handling community concerns fairly.

15

Represent NIGHTFALL DEVILS Properly

When representing the community, act responsibly and respectfully. Your actions can affect the reputation of the entire community.

16

Help Maintain Unity

Encourage friendship, teamwork, support, and positive interaction. The goal is to build the community, not divide it.

👿 MEMBER APPROVAL POLICY

Before someone becomes an official NIGHTFALL DEVILS member, the person must provide their TikTok username, identify the member who referred them, and wait for Admin review.

NO APPROVAL = NO ADDING

🔒 PRIVACY FIRST

Never share another person's private information, personal details, account information, screenshots containing sensitive information, or other confidential matters without permission.

If you are unsure whether something is okay to share, ask Admin first.

READY TO JOIN?

Read and understand the community rules before submitting your membership request.
