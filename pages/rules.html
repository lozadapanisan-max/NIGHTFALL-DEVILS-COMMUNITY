👿 Our Community Rules

NIGHTFALL DEVILS is built around friendship, respect, support, and unity.

01

Respect Everyone

Respect all members, streamers, viewers, and guests. Treat everyone with proper respect regardless of differences.

02

No Below-the-Belt Comments

Avoid personal attacks, insulting remarks, harassment, or comments intended to hurt another person.

03

Do Not Involve Other Communities

Personal issues should not be used to involve or attack another community or group.

04

Support, Don't Bring Down

Support our streamers and members without attacking, insulting, or bringing other people down.

05

Protect Privacy

Never share or disclose another member's personal information without permission.

06

Follow Platform Rules

Follow TikTok's Community Guidelines and all applicable platform rules.

07

Keep Private Matters Private

If there is a concern involving a member, contact an Admin or Moderator privately.

08

No Unnecessary Drama

Keep the community environment positive. Avoid unnecessary arguments and public conflicts.

🛡️ IMPORTANT

A problem involving one member does not automatically represent the entire NIGHTFALL DEVILS community.

⚠️ Handle personal concerns privately.

Please contact an Admin or Moderator instead of creating public conflict, attacking another person, or involving another community.

OUR PRINCIPLE

We are here to build a community, not create unnecessary conflict.

🖤 RESPECT

Respect people even when you disagree.

🤝 UNITY

Support each other and protect the community.

🔥 SUPPORT
