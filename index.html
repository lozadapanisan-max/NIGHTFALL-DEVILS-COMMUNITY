COMMUNITY RULES

Keep NIGHTFALL DEVILS a respectful and positive community.

  1. Respect Everyone — Treat all members, streamers, and communities with respect.
  2. No Below-the-Belt Comments — Avoid personal attacks, insults, harassment, and unnecessary arguments.
  3. Do Not Involve Other Communities — Community issues should be handled privately.
  4. Support, Don't Bring Down — Support our streamers without attacking others.
  5. Protect Privacy — Never disclose or share personal information.
  6. Follow Platform Rules — Follow TikTok and other applicable platform rules.
  7. Keep Private Matters Private — Handle personal issues privately and respectfully.
  8. No Unnecessary Drama — Help keep the community peaceful and positive.
