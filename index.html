👿 Community Rules

Simple rules to keep Nightfall Devils safe, organized, and respectful.

1

Respect Everyone

Respect members, streamers, players, and viewers.

2

No Below-the-Belt Comments

Avoid insults, personal attacks, harassment, and hurtful remarks.

3

Do Not Involve Other Communities

Do not drag other communities into personal issues or conflicts.

4

Support, Don't Bring Down

Support our streamers without attacking or putting others down.

5

Protect Privacy

Never disclose personal information of another member.

6

Follow Platform Rules

Follow TikTok and other platform Community Guidelines.

7

Keep Private Matters Private

Bring community concerns to admins or moderators.

8

No Unnecessary Drama

Keep the GC, comments, and LIVE environment positive.

🔥 New Member Approval

Joining is simple. Admin approval keeps the community organized.

1️⃣ REFER

Send the applicant's TikTok username to an Admin.

2️⃣ REVIEW

Admin checks the basic information.

3️⃣ APPROVED

Only after approval can the member be added officially.

⚠️ No approval = No adding. There is no payment or mandatory contribution required to join.
